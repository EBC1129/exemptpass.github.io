The problem

Your volunteers shouldn't need a briefcase full of paperwork to buy paper towels.

Every time a Texas nonprofit purchases bulk supplies at a store, they are legally required to present a signed paper Form 01-339 at the register. The cashier has usually never seen it. The line backs up. Managers get called. Sometimes the exemption is denied entirely.

This happens millions of times a year across Texas's 74,000 nonprofits — food banks, shelters, veteran service posts, churches, and community organizations that exist to serve people, not stand in checkout lines.

The incumbent solution is a piece of paper. ExemptPass replaces it.

How it works

Set up once. Use everywhere.

A nonprofit registers one time. From that point forward, every authorized buyer has a personal QR code that works at any participating retailer.

1

Nonprofit registers

Upload your IRS determination letter and completed Form 01-339. Our system verifies both against IRS and Texas Comptroller records. Takes 1–3 business days.

2

Buyers get personal QR codes

Your account admin assigns up to 3 authorized buyers. Each receives a personal QR code saved in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or as a printed card. Unique, non-transferable, instantly revocable.

3

Instant verification at checkout

The cashier scans the QR. A green verified screen appears in under 2 seconds — no paper, no manager call, no delay. Every transaction is automatically logged for your records.

Texas pilot & national roadmap

Starting in Texas. Expanding everywhere.

Texas is the right place to start — 74,000 nonprofits, a clear state exemption form, and a reachable Comptroller's office. The framework we build here becomes the model for every other state.

1
Texas Comptroller engagement
Pursuing a Private Letter Ruling to formally establish digital verification authority under Texas Tax Code.
2
Texas pilot launch
25–100 Texas nonprofits onboarded. Live at H-E-B pilot locations across the DFW and Austin metro areas.
3
National expansion
Replicating the Texas framework across all 45 sales-tax states. Each state's exemption certificate mapped and digitized.
4
Federal recognition
Pursuing formal recognition of ExemptPass as an IRS-endorsed digital verification platform for 501(c)(3) organizations nationwide.
